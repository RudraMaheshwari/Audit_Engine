# IM France (Overview) — Business Summary

## Business Overview
- Business Units: Surgery (Ethicon), Orthopedics (DPS), and Cardiovascular (Electrophysiology & Neurovascular).

## Changes and Challenges within the Business
- Organizational transformation in 2023 and 2024, with consequent changes in structure, roles and responsibilities and on-boarding of new leaders: HCCO (after one year vacancy); Medical and ProfEd director (after four-month vacancy); DPS BU Director, Ad Interim Electrophysiology BU Director, Finance Head, and KAM Director.
- Surgery – negative market trend in key specialties (esp. Bariatrics) and acceleration of robotic surgery.
- Orthopedics – reimbursement pressure and austerity (price reductions in fixed-reimbursement products).
- Cardiovascular – disruptive technology entering the market, lack of innovation by J&J.

## Go-to-Market Approach
- FY 2024 NTS of USD 409.3M; forecasted FY 2025 NTS of USD 435M.
- Public vs Private sales: approx. 49% public vs 51% private.
- Distributor sales in FY 2024 were about 2.4% of total sales.
- FY 2024 franchise-wise sales split: Surgery (Ethicon) USD 190M (46.4%), Orthopedics (DPS) USD 103.6M (25.3%), Cardiovascular (CSS) USD 115.7M (28.3%).

## Third-Party Intermediary ("TPI") Overview
- Surgery: Distributors and agents; Cardiovascular: Agents only; Orthopaedics: Distributors and agents.
- Distributors: distribute products to customers in authorized territories and are compensated through rebates.
- Agents: promote and represent products with diligence and maintain relationships with customers in their authorized territories and HCPs.

## Interactions with Public HCPs/GOs/HCIs
- Interactions with public HCPs/GOs/HCOs include FFS, medical education/congresses, educational grants, charitable contributions and tenders. The business utilizes the EMT System and HCC Life System to manage the activities and agreements.

## Key Regulations
- MedTech Europe Code of Ethical Business Practice.
- Public Health Code (Code de la santé publique).
- Anti-Kickback Law.
