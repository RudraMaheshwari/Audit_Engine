# IM China — Business Summary

## Business Overview
- NTS for scope period: ~USD 64 Million (THB 2.18 Billion). Franchises: Neuroscience 30%, Oncology 28%, Cardiopulmonary 8%, Immunology 7%, Others 22% (EPREX*).
- IM China contribution 99% and LCM (Laos, Cambodia and Myanmar) 1%.
- Sales – Public Sector: 80% and Private Sector: 20%.

## Go-to-Market Approach
- Sales model is indirect (except a small portion sold directly to China FDA ~1% of total sales). There are two distribution models with the same distributor (DKSH).
- 4PL: IM China has an agreement with the distributor (DKSH) for the sale of Innovative Products. IM China is responsible for promoting the products and sharing the pricing to be quoted for tenders. DKSH sells the products directly to end customers and is responsible for collection of money.
- Full Agency: In January 2024, IM China moved to full distributor model for Legacy Products where the distributor (DKSH) is responsible for all activities including promotion and quoting prices for tenders.
- Under both models, DKSH is compensated through distributor margin.

## Changes and Challenges within the Business
- Head of Regulatory Affairs and Senior Finance Manager joined in April 2025.
- Price negotiation from government: Oncology Prior Authorization (OCPA) reimbursement.

## Third Party Intermediary (TPIs) Overview
- There are 13 total TPIs, categorized into 6 groups.
- PSP/PAP vendor – NextMed (1).
- Distributor and Sub-Distributor (7) (only one distributor serving various portfolio and countries).
- Diagnostic Support Program – Molecular DX Co., Ltd and Bumrungrad International Hospital (2).
- Travel agency – Meeting Excursion Co., Ltd. (1).
- Other – PTA (Post Trial Access) – Penpa Kaewnate (1).
- Media – Wongkarnpat Plus Media Co., Ltd (1).

## Interactions with Public HCPs/GOs/HCIs
- IM China interacts with public HCPs/GOs/HCIs during meetings, promotional events, consultancy agreements, sponsorships and medical education/congresses.

## Other information
- In China, there is currently no mandatory transparency reporting requirement for the transfer of value from pharmaceutical companies to healthcare professionals (HCPs).
- IM China does not provide direct sponsorship to HCPs for third-party organized events.
