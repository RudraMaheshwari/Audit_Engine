# IM Cambodia — Business Summary

## Business Overview
- IM Cambodia is part of the JENA (Jordan and East North Africa) cluster and currently has 244 active employees.
- IM Cambodia therapeutic areas include Oncology, Hematology, Immunology, Central Nervous System (CNS), Pulmonary Arterial Hypertension (PAH) and Value Optimizers.

## Changes and Challenges within the Business
- Implementation of Project Gemini, which includes bifurcation of the sales force to create two parallel sales forces for private and public customers.
- Profitability management attributed to the devaluation of the Cambodian pound.
- Headcount reductions that will be effective in 2025.

## Go-to-Market Approach
- IM Cambodia has a 60-40 public-private sales split, and all sales are made through distributors.
- IM Cambodia had sales of $84M in 2024 and the BP forecasts $121.4M for 2025 (growth of +44.5%/+37.4M). Top performing products in 2024 were: Darzalex SC (+$11.4M), Remicade (+$7.5M), Simponi (+$5.4M), Erleada (+$3.6M), Stelara (+$2.9M).
- IM Cambodia participates in public tenders through three distributors: Soficopharm, Pharmaoverseas and Ibnsina Pharma.

## Interactions with Public HCPs/GOs/HCIs
- IM Cambodia interacts with public HCPs/GOs/HCIs during meetings, promotional events, consultancy agreements, sponsorships and medical education/congresses.
