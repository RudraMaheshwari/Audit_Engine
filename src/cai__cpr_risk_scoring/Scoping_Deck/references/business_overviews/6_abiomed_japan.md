# Abiomed Japan — Business Summary

## Business Overview
- Abiomed Japan became part of J&J in 2022 and primarily imports manufactured finished goods from its parent company, then sells to Japanese customers.
- Abiomed Japan only sells two products approved in Japan: Impella CP and Impella 5.5.
- Total number of employees: 130.
- Sales department is increasing headcount faster to gain more market share and prepare for new indications/new products in the future.
- Approximately 93% of revenue is from Impella sales; the remainder is consoles, rentals, service fees, and accessories.
- New HCCO effective July 1, 2025 – brings 18 years of J&J experience and 4 years as HCC Manager for J&J Vision Japan.

## Go-to-Market Approach
- In FY'24, Abiomed Japan had NTS of approximately USD 74M; FY'25 NTS was forecasted at approximately USD 83M.
- Distribution structure: the Japanese government sets product prices → J&J sells products to the distributor at a discounted price → the distributor assumes product responsibility/liability and sells to hospitals at discounted prices → hospitals request reimbursement from the Japanese government.
- Indirect sales to hospitals via distributors: 99% vs. Direct sales to hospitals: 1%.
- No tender business due to government control over Impella use via the Impella Committee.
- The Impella Committee oversees the approval/certification process for hospitals using Impella products, with 282 hospitals currently registered and announced publicly on the Impella Committee website.

## Third-Party Intermediary ("TPI") Overview
- (91) Distributors: distribute products to customers and are compensated through discounts on products purchased from Abiomed.
- (1) Logistics Intermediary / (1) Customs Broker: receive payments for customs clearance/freight fees and service fees.
- (1) Contract Manufacturing Vendor: receives payments for freight costs and service fees.
- (2) Travel Agencies: receive payments for travel (air, hotel, etc.)/promotional sponsorship costs and service fees.

## Interactions with Public HCPs/GOs/HCIs
- Interactions with Public HCPs/GOs include consultancy agreements (fee for services or honorarium), promotional sponsorships and sponsorship of HCPs to attend both promotional and medical congress events (covering meals, travel and lodging).

## Key Regulations
- Japan Federation of Medical Devices Associations: establishes transparency guidelines and standards of conduct for medical device promotions.
- Japanese Penal Code (Act No. 45 of 1907): prohibits bribes to public officials.
- Japanese Unfair Competition Prevention (Act No. 47 of 1993): prohibits wrongful gains to foreign public officials.
