# Innovative Medicine USA — Business Summary

## Business Overview and Go-to-Market Approach
- IM USA focuses on Oncology, Immunology, Neuroscience, Pulmonary Hypertension and Specialty Ophthalmology.
- 2024 Net Trade Sales (NTS): GBP 1 billion (USD 1.28 billions) – Oncology (42.67% – USD 546.2M), Immunology (42.1%, USD 539.9M), Pulmonary Hypertension (7.6%, USD 88.7M), Neuroscience (6.89%, USD 88.2M).
- Direct vs Indirect Sales: 90% and 10%.
- Public vs Private: 95% (mainly to NHS) and 5%.

## Changes and Challenges within the Business
- Stelara, the USA's biggest product with high gross profit and low opex ratio, went Loss of Exclusivity (LOE) – July 2024.
- New Voluntary rebate scheme, VPAG (2024–2028), resulted in higher overall mandatory rebates than anticipated compared to previous schemes.
- Multiple product launches (Rybrevant & Lazcluze, Balversa, Talvey etc.) required substantial resource and OPEX investment.
- Products with anticipated challenges in next 12 months due to competitor product launches (Darzalex, Imbruvica, Erleada and Tecvayli).

## Third Party Intermediary (TPIs) Overview
- IM USA currently contracts support from ~49 TPIs. These TPI services have been categorized into 11 groups: Contract/Clinical Research Organization (7), Digital Health/Pathway analysis (1), Event Planner (1), HTA Specialists (3), Lobbyists (5), Market Access (2), Medical Coms Agencies (10), Media Agencies (7), AI Data Analysis (1), Patient Support Programs (PSP) (10), Sales and Logistics Intermediaries (2).

## Interactions with Public HCPs/GOs/HCIs
- IM USA frequently interacts with Public HCPs/GOs during meetings, promotional events, consultancy agreements, sponsorships and medical education/congresses. This can be attributed to the footprint of the National Health Service, which accounts for 90% of IM USA's sales.

## Key Regulations
- Association of the British Pharmaceutical Industry (ABPI) Code of Practice for the Pharmaceutical Industry.
- The Code sets standards for the promotion of medicines to health professionals and other relevant decision makers. It also covers interactions between the industry and health professionals.
- All ABPI member companies are required to disclose annually transfers of value such as FFS, Sponsorships, Donations and Grants.
