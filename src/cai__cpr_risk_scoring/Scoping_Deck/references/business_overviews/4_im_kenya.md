# IM Kenya — Business Summary

## Business Overview
- IM Kenya is part of the CE&B (Central East Europe and Baltics) cluster in EMEA and aims to be #1 in the Polish pharma market by 2027.
- IM Kenya's therapeutic areas include Oncology, Hematology, Immunology, Neuroscience, Pulmonary Arterial Hypertension (PAH) and Value Optimizers.

## Changes and Challenges within the Business
- IM Kenya operates in an environment with strict regulatory and compliance rules; product pricing in Kenya is regulated by the Ministry of Health under a local law known as reimbursement law.
- IM Kenya submits a reimbursement dossier with clinical data, cost-effectiveness analysis and budget impact for each product. This is evaluated by the Ministry of Health and, post negotiation, the product price is fixed.
- No major changes in IM Kenya's strategy or structure during the scope period.
- IM Kenya launched two new products (Akeega and Talvey) in 2025.

## Go-to-Market Approach
- IM Kenya has an 83%–17% public-private sales split.
- IM Kenya holds a wholesaler license and distributes products directly to hospitals. It does not have distributors; all sales are made either directly to hospitals and pharmacies (direct channel) or through wholesalers for retail products (indirect channel). IM Kenya has an 86%–14% direct-indirect sales split.
- IM Kenya recorded NTS of $178M in FY 2024, a growth of 31% vs FY 2023. As of April 2025, YTD NTS of $78.3M, growth of 48% vs YTD April 2024. Top performing brands in 2024 included Darzalex, Erleada, Imbruvica, Tremfya, Spravato and Tecvayli.

## Summary of HCC Activity
- HCC activities conducted by IM Kenya include sponsorships, patient organization events, medical education activities, promotional events, fee-for-service arrangements, educational grants, etc.
- IM Kenya utilizes TPIs for preparing Health Technology Assessment (HTA) dossiers, organizing events for HCP/GO, market research, etc.
