# Insights from GACS Assessment (IM Kenya, 2023)

The following observations were noted from the GACS assessment conducted in 2023.

## Fee for Service
- IM Kenya booked advisory board costs to an incorrect GL account.
- Remuneration paid by IM Kenya to an HCP was above the Fair Market Value range.
- IM Kenya did not obtain and retain sufficient proof of service documentation.
- IM Kenya made payments to an HCP after the due date prescribed in the Fee for Service contract.

## TPI Due Diligence
- IM Kenya did not carry out risk center and Factiva searches while performing due diligence of low risk TPIs.
- IM Kenya signed a TPI contract prior to completion of the due diligence process.
- A contract signed by a TPI did not have a date of signature.
