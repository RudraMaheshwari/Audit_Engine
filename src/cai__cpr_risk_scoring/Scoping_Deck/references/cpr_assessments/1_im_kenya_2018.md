# Prior CPR Assessment — Findings from previous CPR (IM Kenya, 2018)

## Patient Organization Support
- A Patient Organization (PO) signed a contract prior to the activity being approved in iConnect.
- IM Kenya does not evaluate whether the contribution made to a PO is a substantial portion of the PO's budget.
- IM Kenya HCCO did not document in the signed pre-approval form that a PO board member worked at a public hospital, although this was disclosed by the PO.

## Medical Education Events
- Invitation letters provided to HCPs did not contain clauses required by the HCBI guide.
- IM Kenya incurred HCP meal expenses higher than the allowed meal limits.
- IM Kenya did not document the HCP selection criteria for a Congress event.
- IM Kenya made transportation arrangements for an HCP's travel to a Congress prior to the agreement being signed.
- IM Kenya did not request documentation to confirm HCP attendance at a Congress.

## Local Travel Agency
- IM Kenya does not review the detailed post-event reconciliation of HCP travel and meeting expenses provided by the local travel agency.
- Post-event reconciliations provided by the local travel agency did not include sufficient detail.

## Educational Grants
- IM Kenya did not document the rationale for providing Educational Grants in the Educational Grant Review form.
- Grant Committee members did not appropriately complete the Educational Grant Review Form.

## Sponsorships
- Local and EMEA policies do not contain a requirement to obtain and retain proof that services were provided.

## Tenders
- IM Kenya did not retain the final version of the tender offer with approval signatures.
