# Prior Audit Findings — 2021 Commercial CPR (IM China)

## Charitable Contributions
- Critical: Lack of due diligence/background checks on charity board members and directors.
- Major: Company did not document the assessment of whether the contribution was a substantial portion of the recipient organization's budget.

## Fee For Service
- Major: Inconsistencies between the HCPs' key opinion leader status in HCC and their respective curriculum vitaes ("CVs") which resulted in higher honorarium.

## Sponsorships
- Major: Lack of verification that J&J is the sole sponsor of events.
