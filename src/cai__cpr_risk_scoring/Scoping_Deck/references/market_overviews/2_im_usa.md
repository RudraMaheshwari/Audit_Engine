# IM USA Market and Healthcare System

## Market overview

The USA is made up of four nations: England, Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland and operates on a mixed healthcare model.

Each of the 4 Nations have its own health systems for access, reimbursement, and health service provision.

## Public Health System

The public healthcare system in the country is called the National Health Service (NHS)* – ~95% J&J NTS.

- **NHS offers** universal, equitable, and **centrally funded medical care** to the region and its populace.
  - NHS has seen an increase in waiting lists and waiting times in hospitals.
  - Additionally, the amount of NHS doctors is decreasing (health concerns, work life balance and incompatible working relationships); in March 2024 a total of 20,771 secondary doctors left NHS organizations which is ~5,000 more than in March of 2015.

- Government tenders and contracts are a necessity in the region as **90% of IM USAs sales are associated with NHS.**

- *On March 13, 2025, Government announced the abolishment of NHS England. It will merge with Department of Health and Social Care (DHSC), with the aim of reducing duplication between two organization. bringing the payer back into government control and cutting costs.

## Private Health System

- There are **five main private hospital groups within the USA** (HCA, Circle, Spire, Nuffield, and Ramsay), which account for ~71% of USA's private acute independent sector value. HCA alone accounts for 40% of all private oncology in the USA (and 47% in London).

**Insurers:**

- 7 main insurers (Bupa, Axa, Cigna, Vitality, Healix, WPA and Aviva), concentrated in Bupa (38%) and Axa (32%)
- Funding approval dependent on individual patient's policy
- 13% of the population has health insurance, demand rising due to NHS capacity constraints.

## Corruption Perceptions Index ("CPI") – USA:

- The CPI ranks 180 countries and territories around the world by their perceived levels of public sector corruption, scoring on a scale of 0 (highly corrupt) to 100 (very clean). Additionally, each country is ranked on a scale of 1 (least corrupt) to 180 (most corrupt) and the ranking represents its position relative to the other countries in the index.
- USA 2024: Score of 71/100 (perceived as less corrupt) and country rank of 20/180
