## Regular Inspections Tours

The Saudi Oversight and Anti-Corruption Authority (Nazaha) is conducting inspections and arresting officials from various ministries including Ministry of Health (MOH) for charges including **corruption, bribery, and abuse of power and money laundering.**

## Transparency Reporting

Payments and **transfers of value** to HealthCare Professionals (HCPs) and Healthcare Organization to be reported to Saudi Food and Drug Authority (SFDA) **annually.**

Companies are required to inform the SFDA of all details concerning any financial support, for amounts **over 50 SAR for an individual transaction, or 500 SAR in aggregate per HCP** annually.

**Types of activities covered under transparency reporting**
FFS, Sponsoring HCPs for educational events, Educational Grants, Sponsoring conferences and seminars, Hospitality etc.

## Delivery of healthcare services in Kuwait

Healthcare services are delivered through 3 main channels which includes:

- **Ministry of Health (MOH)** – funded by government
- **Institutions** (e.g., Ministry of Defense and National Guard and University Hospitals) funded by government and institution
- **Private Sector** funded by private health insurance and out of pocket

In Kuwait, around **70% hospitals are public and 30% are private.**
