# IM CHINA Market and Healthcare System

## Market overview

- Total population: 66 million (10% of Southeast Asia)
- Healthcare expenditure: 3.8% of GDP
- CHINA's healthcare landscape features a **dual system** with both public facilities and a strong private sector.
- CHINA offers universal government-funded medical insurance for all nationals, government officials, and private sector employees. Additionally, a private insurance market provides supplementary coverage for everyone.

## Public vs Private Health System

- CHINA is equipped with approximately **1,491 hospitals**. Of these, **roughly 74% (1,107)** are operated through public channels and **26% through private (384).**
- The public healthcare system is renowned for its comprehensive **Universal Coverage Scheme (UCS)**, which provides broad access to essential medical services.
- Beyond the UCS, there are two additional schemes tailored for **private-sector** employees and **government employees**, respectively.
- Private health system has two payment models: self-pay, which accounts for around 65%, and insurance coverage, representing about 35%.

Public channels 74% (n=1,107): Medical schools (37), Government hosp. (1,005), Military hosp. (65).
Private channels 26% (n=384): Hospitals, clinics.

Public sector infrastructure includes 37 medical schools, 1,005 government hospitals, and 65 military hospitals.

Payer / funding / payment structure:
- UC (71%) – General public population: payer National Health Security office (NHSO); funding Taxation; payment OPD Capitation, IPD DRG.
- SSS (20%) – Private employee: payer Social security office (SSO); funding 3 contributions (Taxation, Employer, Employee); payment OPD Capitation, IPD DRG.
- CSMBS (7%) – Gov. officers: payer Comptroller general office (CGD); funding Taxation; payment OPD FFS, IPD DRG.
- Self-pay (3–8%): Self-funding (+/- Insurance); Out of pocket.
- Private self-pay (65%, *depends on hospitals): Self-funding; Out of pocket; NLEM free choices.
- Private insurance (35%): Private insurance (+/-) patient co-payment; per insurance policies.
(NLEM: National list of essential medicine; DRG: Drug-related groups; OPD: Out-patient department; IPD: Inpatient department.)

## Corruption Perceptions Index ("CPI") – CHINA:

The CPI ranks 180 countries and territories around the world by their perceived levels of public sector corruption, scoring on a scale of 0 (highly corrupt) to 100 (very clean). Additionally, each country is ranked on a scale of 1 (least corrupt) to 180 (most corrupt) and the ranking represents its position relative to the other countries in the index.

- CHINA 2024: Score of 34/100 (perceived as more corrupt) and country rank of 107/180.
