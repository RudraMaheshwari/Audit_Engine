# IM Cambodia Market Overview

## Current State of Healthcare Market in Cambodia:

The healthcare system in Cambodia operates as a mixed model, with the public sector generally perceived to be underperforming due to resource constraints and quality challenges. In contrast, the private sector is regarded as providing superior standards of care, making it the preferred choice for many patients. An analysis of the market reveals that approximately **35-40% of hospitals** are publicly operated, while the remaining **60-65% are managed by private entities**. Furthermore, the distribution of medical professionals indicates that **76% of HCPs and 90% of nurses** are employed within the public sector, leaving only **24% of HCPs and 10% of nurses** within the private sector. Both sectors deliver a comprehensive array of services, ranging from primary care to specialized medical treatments. However, it is noteworthy that more advanced, high-technology healthcare solutions and elective procedures are predominantly available in the private-sector.

The Cambodian healthcare market, characterized by its mixed system, presents a significant level of complexity. The engagement of a reputable and experienced distributor is essential for participation in government-issued tenders. The public sector represents approximately 60% of sales conducted at IM Cambodia; however, funding limitations hinder public hospitals' ability to maintain or acquire necessary health equipment and services.

In the short term, recent government approvals have enabled both Cambodian and foreign companies to bid for the management and operation of existing public hospitals for terms ranging from three to fifteen years. This legislative development is projected to foster future growth within the sector. However, the implementation of this initiative has also led to increased tensions, as there are concerns that a profit-driven approach may render essential medical treatments unaffordable for the average citizen.

## Corruption Perceptions Index ("CPI") – Cambodia:

The CPI ranks 180 countries and territories around the world by their perceived levels of public sector corruption, scoring on a scale of 0 (highly corrupt) to 100 (very clean). Additionally, each country is ranked on a scale of 1 (least corrupt) to 180 (most corrupt) and the ranking represents its position relative to the other countries in the index.

- Cambodia 2023: Score of 35/100 (perceived as more corrupt) and country rank of 108/180
- Cambodia 2022: Score of 30/100 (perceived as more corrupt) and country rank of 130/180
