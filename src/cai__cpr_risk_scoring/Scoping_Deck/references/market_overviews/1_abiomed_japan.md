# Abiomed Japan Market Overview

## Current State of Healthcare Market in Japan:

- There are 2 main types of health insurance (private insurance is not widely used):

  **Kokumin Kenkou Hoken (国民健康保険) or National Health Insurance (NHI)**
  - Used by individuals who are self-employed; unemployed; retirees; business owners; freelancers
  - Co-payments for injuries/sickness are usually 20%–30% and calculated based on income
  - Includes mandatory Long-term Care Insurance (LTCI) for individuals aged 65+

  **Shakai Hoken (社会保険) or Employee-Based Insurance (EHI)**
  - Employer-provided health insurance for full-time workers (about 59% of population)
  - Insurance typically covers 70% of costs, leaving a 30% co-payment

- Public Hospitals: 55.7% vs. Private Hospitals: 44.3%
- Japan's medical device and materials market is among the world's largest: in 2024, it was valued at approximately $27.2 billion USD.
- The market is expected to reach USD 54.6 Billion by 2033, exhibiting a CAGR of 8.1% from 2025–2033.
- The market heavily relies on imports, especially for sophisticated devices such as pacemakers, PTCA catheters and orthopedic implants.
- Japan's aging population, with over 36.25 million elderly people in 2024, is driving the expanding home healthcare market.
- Individuals aged 65+ constitute ~30% of the population, the highest globally for countries with over 100,000 residents.
- The rising prevalence of chronic diseases like cardiovascular issues and diabetes further fuels market growth.
- Although not related to any Healthcare companies, seven FCPA enforcement actions against Japanese companies and/or related entities occurred since 2011, netting approximately USD 705 Million.

## Corruption Perceptions Index ("CPI") – Japan:

The CPI ranks 180 countries and territories around the world by their perceived levels of public sector corruption, scoring on a scale of 0 (highly corrupt) to 100 (very clean). Additionally, each country is ranked on a scale of 1 (least corrupt) to 180 (most corrupt) and the ranking represents its position relative to the other countries in the index.

- Japan 2024: Score of 71 (perceived as less corrupt) and country rank of 20
- Japan 2023: Score of 73 (perceived as less corrupt) and country rank of 16
