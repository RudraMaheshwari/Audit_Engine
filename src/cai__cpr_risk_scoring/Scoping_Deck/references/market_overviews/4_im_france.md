# Overview (IM France)

## I. Current State of Healthcare Market

Public AND private hospitals are **publicly funded.** As of 2023 there were **2,976** hospitals. Of these facilities:
- 45% were public
- 33% private for-profit
- 22% private non-profit hospitals

For-profit (Private) hospitals make up 58% of the surgical* activity
- More than 430 hospitals with an activity in surgery
- 5.9M surgical hospital stays in 2023 (+8.4% vs. 2019)

Non-profit (Public/ESPIC) 42% of the surgical* activity
- More than 500 hospitals with an activity in surgery
- 4.3M surgical hospital stays in 2023 (+7.3% vs. 2019)

*surgical activity = surgical + interventional (with less invasive procedures) stays

## II. Key notions

- Allocation of about 12.1% of its total GDP to healthcare. In comparison, the US spends around 16.6%.
- All residents must have health insurance, and the Social Security system is the primary system for most.
- Excess costs above the conventional tariff (amount negotiated between HCPs and Social Security) are left to the patient or covered by private insurance.
- Supplemental health insurance is offered by private insurance companies
- Enrollment in complementary insurance is optional; however, employers in the private sector are required to provide it for their employees.
- Price of medical devices is negotiated when listed in the LPPR. Negotiation is between the CEPS (Healthcare Products Pricing Committee) and the manufacturer.

## III. Corruption Perceptions Index ("CPI"):

The CPI ranks 180 countries and territories around the world by perceived levels of public sector corruption, scoring 0 (highly corrupt) to 100 (very clean). Each country is ranked on a scale of 1 (least corrupt) to 180 (most corrupt), representing its position relative to the other countries in the index.

- 2024: Score of 67 (more corrupt) and country rank of 25 (more corrupt)
